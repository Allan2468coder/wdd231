NP Membership Benefits

  • No membership fee or dues.
  • Chamber event invitations and community partnership access.
  • Listing in the nonprofit chamber directory.
  • Support for mission-focused outreach and visibility.

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Featured listing on the chamber site.
  • Monthly member newsletter and event announcements.
  • Access to marketing resources and small business development content.
  • Participation in networking mixers.

Silver Membership Benefits

  • Rotating spotlight feature on the chamber homepage.
  • Discounts on chamber-hosted seminars and training sessions.
  • Priority access to sponsorship opportunities.
  • Member-to-member referral support.

Gold Membership Benefits

  • Premium homepage spotlight and dedicated member showcase.
  • Complimentary booth space at the next chamber trade fair.
  • Advanced business training and leadership roundtables.
  • High visibility advertising across chamber communications.
