GrowWell Farm Technologies Site Plan

Site Name

GrowWell Farm Technologies was selected because it communicates healthy growth and useful technology for modern, practical farmers.

Site Purpose

The site provides a digital library of practical guides for small growers. Visitors can explore crop, soil, water, protection, and planning resources; view details; and save useful guides for later.

Scenarios

  • Which guide can help me use water efficiently during a dry season?
  • How can I protect vegetable crops sustainably?
  • Can I save useful growing guides and return to them later?

Color Scheme

Evergreen
Headings, navigation, buttons, and footer.
Leaf Lime
Brand mark and calls to action.
Harvest Sun
Warm process accents.
Field Mist
Quiet section backgrounds.

Typography and Wireframe

Fraunces is used for expressive headings, Manrope for labels and navigation, and DM Sans for readable body copy. The mobile layout stacks the hero and guide cards. Wider screens use a two-column hero and three-column grids for guide cards and statistics.
