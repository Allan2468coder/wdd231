GrowWell Farm Technologies Site Plan

A responsive agriculture-technology site that gives small growers practical, clear digital field guides.

Site Name

GrowWell Farm Technologies

The name communicates healthy growth and useful technology. It was chosen for a friendly, dependable identity that fits growers looking for modern but practical farming guidance.

Suggested domain: growwellfarm.tech

Site Purpose

GrowWell Farm Technologies provides a digital library of practical agriculture guides for small growers. Visitors can explore crop, soil, water, protection, and planning resources; view guide details; and save useful guides for later.

Scenarios

  • Which guide can help me use water more efficiently during a dry season?
  • How can I find simple, sustainable ways to protect my vegetable crops?
  • Can I save useful growing guides and return to them later?

Color Scheme

Evergreen — #173F2C
Headings, navigation, primary buttons, and footer.
Leaf Lime — #B8D55A
Brand mark and high-energy calls to action.
Harvest Sun — #F5BA45
Process accents and warm highlights.
Field Mist — #EDF3E6
Quiet content-section backgrounds.

Typography

Fraunces is used for expressive, agriculture-focused headings. Manrope is used for labels, navigation, and short headings. DM Sans is used for readable body copy, forms, and guide data.

Wireframe

The Home page uses a responsive layout: mobile stacks the brand, hero, guide controls, and guide cards in a single readable column. Wider screens use a two-column hero and three-column grids for statistics and field-guide cards. The supplied wireframe influenced the page hierarchy: navigation, strong hero, trust content, useful cards, call to action, and footer.
