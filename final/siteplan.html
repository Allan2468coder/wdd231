Smokey Technologies Site Plan

A plan for a friendly, professional website design studio serving small businesses that need a strong online home.

Site Name

Smokey Technologies

The name was selected because it feels memorable, modern, and energetic. “Technologies” immediately communicates a digital service, while “Smokey” adds a distinct, approachable brand personality.

Suggested domain: smokeytechnologies.com

Site Purpose

Smokey Technologies provides website design and development services for small businesses. The site explains its responsive website, accessibility, and site-improvement services, shows its people-first approach, and gives prospective clients a clear way to make contact.

Scenarios

  • How can a new website help my small business earn customers' trust?
  • Will my website work well on phones as well as desktop computers?
  • How do I contact Smokey Technologies to discuss my project?

Color Scheme

Deep Navy — #12324A
Used for headings, navigation, primary buttons, and the footer.
Ocean Blue — #176B87
Used for eyebrow labels, focus accents, and supporting emphasis.
Warm Coral — #EE7858
Used for the logo mark and process accents.
Soft Sky — #E8F6FA
Used for calm section backgrounds and icon surfaces.

Typography

Space Grotesk is used for headings and the brand to give the site a clear, confident voice. DM Sans is used for body text, links, buttons, and form controls because it is highly readable at small sizes. Both fonts are used throughout this document and the completed website.

Wireframe

The following simplified structural sketches document the supplied home-page wireframe’s responsive intent: a compact, single-column mobile layout and a wider, two-column desktop layout. The finished website follows this structure with the project’s actual colors, images, and typography.
